## 👨‍💻 About Me

Hi! I'm **Mohammed Adil**, an Electronics and Communication Engineering undergraduate at **KPR Institute of Engineering and Technology (KPRIET), Coimbatore, India**.

I'm interested in the intersection of **electronics, embedded software, and connected devices**—from microcontrollers and sensor interfaces to IoT systems that collect and present useful data.

- 🎓 ECE undergraduate at KPRIET
- 🔧 Exploring embedded systems, microcontrollers, sensors, and IoT
- 💻 Working with MATLAB, Arduino, C, and technical tools
- 🌱 Continuously learning through projects, labs, and hands-on experimentation
- 🤝 Interested in collaborating on practical ECE / embedded / IoT projects
